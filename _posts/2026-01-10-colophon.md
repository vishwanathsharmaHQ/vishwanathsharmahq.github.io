date: 2026-01-10

This site is a small static Jekyll site hosted on GitHub Pages. Posts live as Markdown files in `_posts/`. The shelf you see is generated at build time from those files — no framework, no client-side rendering, no database.

The typeface is whatever Georgia's fallback stack finds on your machine. If you'd prefer to read it in your own preferred serif, you can override it with a browser stylesheet. I've tried to leave the styles simple enough that this is not a fight.

The layout is a horizontal shelf of vertical spines. Click one and it opens. Scroll inside. Click it again, or click a different spine, to close it. The design is meant to feel like a shelf of books rather than a stream of posts. It rewards browsing over scrolling.

The source is on GitHub. If you find a typo, you can open a pull request, and the odds are good I'll merge it before I've finished my next coffee. This is not a magazine. It's a shelf. Contributions welcome.
