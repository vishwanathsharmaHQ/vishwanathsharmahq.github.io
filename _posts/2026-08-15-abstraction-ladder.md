As a self-taught developer with no formal CS education, It has always bugged me that I don't 'actually' understand what is going on. I am in my cozy little garbage collected, memory managed abstraction land playing my @decorator this @decorator that, library function call, utility function, class, inheritence, parameter `**kwargs`, type checking linting all tests passing games, collecting my paycheck and going home. But when I lie down in my bed at night, alone with my thoughts, I wonder, but how does it all work?

And this is not about 'imposter-syndrome', it is the feeling of 'I don't understand how it works end to end', At some point it becomes a black box. How the fuck do we dig a mineral out of the ground and make it do all this? How do we make the earth think for us? And now with LLMs, how do we get to a point where I am talking to a piece of the earth? It's fucking magic and I would like to know how it is performed, please sir magician.

And so here I am up on this ladder of abstraction, in the land of the web. Do I climb down the rungs slowly? Where does that get me? To chromium? v8? Fuck that. It won't work anyway. If I want to understand (and maybe this is just how my brain works) I gotta start at the start.

Now considering that I can't possibly mine my own silicon and build chips, let me start at the point after that. I have a raspberry pi lying around. I'm gonna try building an OS. Nothing serious, just enough to understand how it works.

This is probably going to be a long, arduous process and I intend to document it, mostly for myself, but since I have this website and have always wanted to have a blog on it, might as well put it up here. If anyone is interested...
