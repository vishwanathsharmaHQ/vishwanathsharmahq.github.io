This blog is a bookshelf. Each title on the left is a spine. Click one and it opens into a room you can read in. Scroll inside the room. Close it when you're done and pick another. The metaphor is not a metaphor. Books really are like this — closed until they aren't, silent until they aren't, and then talkative in a way that follows you around for years.

I have wanted a site like this for a long time. Something I could type into. Something that didn't demand a headline. A place where a paragraph could sit next to a poem next to a quarter-finished argument and none of them would feel out of place. If you're reading this, that's what you've walked into. Take your shoes off. Wander around.

I don't know how often I'll write. I know I'll write more when I have somewhere to put it. A wooden shelf makes you buy books. A blank page makes you write. This is my blank page, angled like a shelf, waiting to fill up. If the shelves look empty in a few weeks, forgive me — real life has a way of taking over. I'll be back.

Everything here is written slowly. Nothing is meant to be finished. If a piece feels unresolved, that's probably because it is; if it feels certain, distrust that. Certainty on the internet has a short half-life. What lasts is a real voice, willing to be wrong out loud.

You'll find drafts here, and half-drafts, and things that are just a sentence long. I've stopped believing in the tyranny of the "publishable" post. Some of the best writing on the web is a paragraph. Some of the worst is ten thousand words. I'll try to be short when I have nothing to say, and long when I do.
