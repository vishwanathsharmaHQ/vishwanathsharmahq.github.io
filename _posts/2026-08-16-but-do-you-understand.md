title: "But do you understand it?"
date: 2026-08-16

Do people really believe that hearing someone else's thoughts on a subject matter is equivalent to ingesting and digesting the source material yourself? You listen to a couple of youtube videos that have distilled a half dozen books into a neat concise essay and you think you understand it?

People who have a fairly high verbal IQ and comprehension are particularly susceptible to this. You listen to a video and the person makes a highly sophisticated argument and it all 'makes sense' to you. You are able to parse it in real time and decide that you agree/disagree with it, and after a few listens, you'll be able to regurgitate most of it in a fairly convincing manner. But do you understand it? What are you optimising for?

You get the feeling of understanding something complex without putting in the work to understand it. You confuse *being able to follow a well structured argument* with *understanding the subject matter deeply*; to say nothing of the biases of the distiller that have been baked into your second hand understanding. Some thirty minutes after you watch the youtube video if you were to debate someone on the same topic you'd do a terrible job.

But it doesn't matter, cause no one is putting in the effort now and so you don't get caught. You can float through the world without ever deeply understanding what you are saying because no one can catch your bluff. Everyone is a copy of a copy of someone else doing the work. Why is it that when I get into it with someone nowadays, I can tell three arguments in what the next fifteen are going to be? You're lazy, you like feeling smart without putting in the effort. You regurgitate half understood arguments with passion and pretend to understand it. You're an imposter. 

AI is making all this so much worse. Now you can do tangible work without understanding anything. Now you *don't even have to look at the code bro*. Why think? Claude will do it for you. Why remember anything? Claude will do it for you.Why gain expertise in anything when Claude can do it for you?  
 
You're trading your intelligence for a subscription service that sells it back to you.

Do you understand?
